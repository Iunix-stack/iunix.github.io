AIM ASSIST

FOLLOW points the gun where you walk, and holds the diagonal you were on when you let go instead of snapping back to a cardinal direction. STICKY is the same but freezes the gun the instant you stop moving. TURN KEYS takes your aim off your feet completely: Q E for player 1, , . for player 2, aim at any angle you like and walk wherever you want.

Aim assist finishes the last couple of degrees for you when your opponent is already close to your line of fire. Brackets appear round them when it has hold. Turn it off for a straight fight.

Hold the lock key and you keep pointing where you are while you walk anywhere: C for player 1, R-CTRL for player 2. Dash is SHIFT and R-SHIFT, on a short cooldown.
