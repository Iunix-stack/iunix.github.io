Good questions.

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. You get a direct reply, usually the same day.

How long will my website take?

Once we have your logo, photos and business information, a simple website can usually be ready within a few days. Bigger websites and Studio builds take a little longer, and we agree the timeline with you up front.

What do I need to send before we start?

Your business name, a logo if you have one, photos of your work, your services and prices, contact number, location, and any websites or colours you like. You don't need everything perfect: send what you have and we'll help organise the rest.

What if I don't have a domain yet?

That's completely fine. We help you choose a domain and connect everything so your website works properly when it goes live.

Can I host my website with IUNIX?

Yes. We handle hosting for you from R175/month, depending on what your website needs.

Can I update the website later?

Yes. If you need to change prices, photos, wording, services or contact details after launch, we can help with that.

Will it look good on a phone?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, so it looks clean and is easy to use on a phone as well as a computer.
